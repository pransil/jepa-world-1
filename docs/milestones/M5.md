# M5: Goal and pixel heads

- **Completed:** 2026-11-17   **PRs:** #11 (standalone modules), this wiring PR

## Built
- `jepa_world/models/goal.py`:
  - `GoalHead` takes pooled L3 (optionally + L2) and gives a diagonal 2-D Gaussian over the
    ball's displacement from t (world m), per goal horizon. The log-variance is bounded,
    b·tanh(raw/b);
  - `goal_nll` gives one NLL per horizon;
  - `displacement_targets` makes the labels from `ball_pos`.
- `jepa_world/models/pixel.py`:
  - `PixelDecoder` is the U-Net decoder path (L3 → L2 → L1, with skips) → the RGB frame at t+1,
    in [0, 1]. It has a `skips` switch for M7;
  - `pixel_l1` is the loss.
- `jepa_world/models/losses.py`: `gaussian_nll_terms`, the one NLL formula, shared by the far
  heads and the goal head.
- `jepa_world/models/world.py`:
  - `WorldModel` is a JEPA subclass, so pre-M5 checkpoints still load. It adds the aux heads for
    the `goal` / `pixel` stages, and they read the context levels only;
  - `compute_world_losses` gives the JEPA terms + `loss/goal/k<k>`, `goalerr/k<k>`,
    `goalratio/k<k>`, `loss/goal`, `loss/pix`.
- `jepa_world/train.py`:
  - the `goal` and `pixel` stages, with `stage_start` and `active_aux`;
  - `with_gt` batches when the goal stage is on, and `batch_k_max` covers the goal horizons;
  - `train_step` takes a frames tensor or a dict.
- `jepa_world/data/synthetic.py`: `moving_disc_batch(..., with_gt=True)` adds a frame-unit
  `ball_pos`, for smoke runs.

## Measured
- **Not yet on MPS.** The step-time cost of the aux heads at SPEC shapes is unmeasured. The GPU
  was held by the first real training run (`wt-exp-first-real-run`) for this whole lane. The
  `@mps` test shows the full four-stage step runs, finite, with no CPU fallback. It doesn't time
  it. `pix_widths: [64, 32]` was chosen by analogy with the benchmarked L1 head width, not from a
  measurement (see the decision).
