## Hypothesis
The 24k headline's goal head was still improving at step 24,000: on training batches,
`goalratio` was near 1.0 until about step 12,000, then fell through 0.50–0.60 (16k–20k) to
0.39–0.48 (20k–24k). **Hypothesis:** doubling the training to 48,000 steps lowers the goal head's
k = 20 error ratio to persistence on test, from 0.430 (mean of two seeds) at 24k.

**Deciding metric:** goal-head mean position error at k = 20 as a ratio to persistence, on the
**test** split, mean of seeds 0 and 1, at step 48,000.
- Scored by `uv run python -m jepa_world.eval --run <run> --split test --split dev`, with the
  same windows (t ∈ 3..39) and baselines as the 24k headline.
- Dev numbers are reported separately (SPEC §7.3).

**Supported if** both seeds' k = 20 test ratio is below both 24k seeds' (0.448 / 0.412).
**Refuted if** the mean is at or above 0.430. In between is inconclusive.

**Also reported:**
- every k's ratio to persistence on test; k = 1 / 2 against constant velocity (24k: 2.36 / 1.20);
- calibration (coverage at 50% / 90%) and the visibility `std_ratio`;
- near-head relerr over the whole run: does the drift after 6000 continue (24k: k5 0.36, k1 0.11)?
- whether the goal head is still improving at 48k (training `goalratio` per 4k window);
- training sanity: every term finite, embedding std per level, `loss/var`.

**Baselines:** persistence, constant velocity, and the simulator with mean parameters (the
physics bar), with true parameters as the oracle; all from eval, on the same windows.

**Budget:** 2 seeds × 48,000 steps, about 2 h per seed on MPS (the 24k runs took about 62 min),
plus one eval per seed, never alongside another job.

**Reproducibility check, free:** there is no learning-rate schedule, and training on MPS was
bitwise deterministic in the guard comparison's check (2100 steps, every term identical). So
steps 1–24,000 of each seed should match the 24k headline run's `metrics.jsonl` exactly. If they
do, the 48k run is a pure continuation of the 24k one.

## Results

### Reproducibility: steps 1–24,000 are the 24k runs
For both seeds, every logged term of the first 24,000 steps in `metrics.jsonl` is bitwise identical
to the 24k headline run with the same seed: same keys, maximum absolute difference 0. Steps
24,001–48,000 are a pure continuation of those runs, so the 24k → 48k differences below come from
the extra training alone.

- **The goal head became more overconfident.** Its errors fell, but its predicted spread fell
  further. At k ≥ 5, the 90% interval holds 79–82% of truths, against 85–89% at 24k. The 50%

- **Uncertainty still rises when the ball is out of view.** Predicted std is 4.0–4.8× higher when
  the ball is hidden (seed 0), and 5.4–6.5× (seed 1), at k ≤ 10. At 24k it was 3.6–4.9×.
- **Hidden-ball coverage stays low.** The 90% interval holds 68–87% (s0) and 80–88% (s1) of truths
  when the ball is hidden, against 72–90% at 24k.

### Training (train split, MPS, B = 32, about 1 h 55 min per seed)
`goalratio` is the goal head's error ÷ persistence's on training batches (mean over each 4k window):

| steps | k1 s0 / s1 | k5 s0 / s1 | k20 s0 / s1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 16,001–20,000 | 0.603 / 0.591 | 0.501 / 0.476 | 0.603 / 0.549 |
| 20,001–24,000 | 0.497 / 0.480 | 0.390 / 0.369 | 0.483 / 0.433 |
| 28,001–32,000 | 0.415 / 0.416 | 0.311 / 0.303 | 0.391 / 0.373 |
| 36,001–40,000 | 0.384 / 0.375 | 0.283 / 0.278 | 0.359 / 0.346 |
| 40,001–44,000 | 0.371 / 0.362 | 0.273 / 0.269 | 0.347 / 0.339 |
| 44,001–48,000 | 0.359 / 0.347 | 0.266 / 0.262 | 0.340 / 0.330 |

**The goal head is still improving at 48k, but slowly.** On training batches, k20 fell 0.48 / 0.43
→ 0.39 / 0.37 over the first 8k extra steps, and only 0.347 / 0.339 → 0.340 / 0.330 over the last 4k.

Near heads and spread (`relerr`, 1.0 = no better than the batch mean target):

| steps | relerr k1 s0 / s1 | relerr k5 s0 / s1 | relerr k20 s0 / s1 | emb std L1 / L3 (s0) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 23,001–24,000 | 0.111 / 0.107 | 0.362 / 0.362 | 0.583 / 0.564 | 1.84 / 1.88 |
| 35,001–36,000 | 0.126 / 0.121 | 0.382 / 0.384 | 0.574 / 0.554 | 1.90 / 1.97 |
| 47,001–48,000 | 0.138 / 0.137 | 0.398 / 0.406 | 0.560 / 0.539 | 1.91 / 1.99 |

- **The near heads keep drifting worse** for the whole second half: k5 0.36 → 0.40, k1 0.11 → 0.14.
  The far k20 head keeps improving (0.58 / 0.56 → 0.56 / 0.54).
- That drift doesn't stop the goal head improving. See the guard comparison
  ([2026-11-18-guard-comparison.md](2026-11-18-guard-comparison.md)) for how the variance guard is
  involved.

**Sanity:**
- Every term is finite in both seeds over all 48,000 steps.
- No collapse: embedding std is 1.9–2.0 at every level by the end, and `loss/var` about 0.08.
- The pixel term stays effectively absent: `w_pix · loss/pix` is at most 3.2e-5 of the rest of
  the loss (mean about 1.5e-7).

## Conclusion
**Supported.** At 48,000 steps the goal head's k = 20 error on the test terrains is 0.353 / 0.347
of persistence's (mean **0.350**), against 0.448 / 0.412 (mean 0.430) at 24,000 steps. Both 48k
seeds are below both 24k seeds. Every other horizon improves too (15–27%), and k = 2 now beats
constant velocity.

Two costs come with it:
- **Calibration got worse.** The 90% intervals hold 79–82% of truths at k ≥ 5, against 85–89%
  at 24k.
- **The near heads kept regressing**, to k5 relerr 0.40.

The goal head is still improving on training batches at 48k, but by only 2–3% per 4k steps. The
mean-parameter simulator is still far ahead: its error is 3.5–24× smaller.

## Next (proposals for the user; none run)
1. **Recalibrate the goal head's variance.** One scale factor per horizon, fit on dev, applied to
   the predicted std; then report test coverage.
   - It is a post-hoc eval option, needing no retraining (a small code change through
     /builder).
   - It would tell whether the overconfidence is a global scale (easy to fix) or depends on the
     window, e.g. on the ball being hidden (needs modelling work).
2. **Longer training: 96k steps** only if the user wants the asymptote. The last 4k steps gained
   about 2% on training batches, and a 96k run is about 4 h per seed.
3. **Make the 48k config the reference for M7 ablations?** The M7 ablations (pixel share, skips,
   camera, top-down, obstacles) are planned at 24k.
   - At 24k the goal head is still on its steep part, so ablation differences mix with
     "how far along the learning curve".
   - 48k would cost twice the GPU time per arm. The user's call.
