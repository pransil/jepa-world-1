## What the switch does
With `unet_skips: false`, the pixel decoder upsamples from L3 alone, and L1/L2 feed it nothing
(`models/pixel.py`). So the pixel loss reaches the encoder only through L3, and the decoder has
fewer input channels.

The pixel term stays at 10% of the rest of the loss in both arms, through `pix_share`. With the
skips off, `loss/pix` should rise and `w_pix_eff` fall to hold the share. The arms differ in what
the decoder must get from L3, not in the pixel term's share of the loss.

## Hypothesis and rules (pre-registered)
**With the skips off, the pixel loss can only be met through L3, so L3 holds more ball-position
information.**

- **Deciding metric:** the L3 linear probe's ball-position error on the **test** split.
  - This is eval's `pos_cam` probe on pooled L3. `pos_cam` is the ball's position relative to the
    camera; world `pos` isn't readable, because the camera tracks the ball.
  - The error is the Euclidean RMSE over x and z, in metres:
    `experiments/m7_ablation_check.py --deciding probe_l3_pos_cam`.
  - Reference: **0.1733 / 0.1921 m** (R² 0.925 / 0.907).
- **Verdict:**
  - **supported** if both skips-off seeds are below both reference seeds, i.e. both under 0.1733;
  - **refuted** if not: within the seed spread, or worse (M7-plan §2).
- **Sanity check that the switch acted:** `loss/pix` over the last 1,000 steps is higher with the
  skips off, in both seeds.
- **Reported, no prediction:**
  - goal head k = 1–20 against the reference, with the same both-seeds rule;
  - the deciding horizon k = 20, to see whether a richer L3 helps or costs far prediction;
  - dev, separately;
  - const-velocity and the mean-parameter simulator;
  - calibration and out-of-view behaviour;
  - the other probes: `pos_cam` at L1, L2 and pooled, plus `radius`, `pos`, friction and
    restitution;
  - training-batch `relerr`, `emb_std`, the far `logvar`, `w_pix_eff`, and `goalratio` per 4k.

**Training-sanity gates**, before any conclusion: the reference's A1–A5 thresholds, applied by the
scorer.
- every term finite;
- `emb_std` never below 50% of its step-2,000 value;
- far `logvar` averaging −3 or more over the last 500 steps, and never below −8;
- `pix_share` within 1e-4 of 0.10;
- `w_goal_eff` = 0.5 / 256.

A run that fails a gate is reported, and not used for a verdict.

## Procedure
One job at a time, each only with no other project python job (`tools/gpu-status.sh`) and ≥ 10 GB
free. The worktree stays clean throughout.
1. Train s0: `uv run python -m jepa_world.train --config configs/m7_skips_off.yaml --name m7-skips-off-s0`.

4. Score: `uv run python experiments/m7_ablation_check.py --arm <s0> --arm <s1> --ref <0f2411> --ref <ada237> --deciding probe_l3_pos_cam`.

**Budget:** 2 × 48,000 steps at about 1.8 h each, plus 2 evals of about 3 min: ≈ 4 GPU-h.

## Conclusion
**Refuted.** With the decoder skips off, L3 holds **less** ball-position information, not more. The
L3 `pos_cam` probe's test RMSE is 0.2012 / 0.2069 m, against the reference's 0.1733 / 0.1921, so
both seeds are worse. Pooled `pos_cam` is worse too (0.183 / 0.188 against 0.158 / 0.174). L1 and L2
are within the seed spread.

The goal head pays for it at every horizon:
- test error ÷ persistence is worse at k = 1–20 in both seeds;
- the deciding horizon k = 20 is 0.393 / 0.380, against 0.329 / 0.331 (+17%);
- k = 1 is +30%;
- dev agrees at every horizon.

**What the training curves add (description, not a test):**
- **Early on, the skips-off runs escaped persistence sooner.** Training-batch k1 `goalratio` over
  steps 12,001–16,000 was 0.694 / 0.720, against 0.927 / 0.925.
- **They then plateaued higher.** Over 44,001–48,000 it was 0.285 / 0.292, against 0.227 / 0.263.
- **One latent term moved the other way.** The k10 latent `relerr` is lower with the skips off
  (0.514 / 0.495 against 0.567 / 0.549), while k20 is higher.
- **So routing the pixel loss through L3 changed training, but not towards position in L3.** What
  L3 holds instead isn't measured here. `relerr` is relative to each run's own EMA targets, so it
  doesn't compare encodings directly.

**Also reported:**
- **90% calibration at long horizons is closer to nominal with the skips off.** At k = 20 it is
  0.90 / 0.90, against 0.83 / 0.88; at k = 1 the 50% coverage is lower (0.51 / 0.49 against 0.58 /
  0.61).
- **The out-of-view std rise at k = 1 is smaller in both seeds,** but still well above 1: 5.6 / 7.1,
  against 8.4 / 7.7.

## Next
- **No change to the defaults.** The skips stay on (`unet_skips: true`).
- **Not proposed: a 30% pixel-share arm with the skips off.** M7-plan lists it as optional, and this
  result doesn't motivate it. A larger pixel share would push more of the loss through the same
  route, which here made L3's position code worse.
- **For M7's write-up:** the skips do not starve L3. With them on, L3 carries more position, and the
  goal head, which reads L3, is better at every horizon.
